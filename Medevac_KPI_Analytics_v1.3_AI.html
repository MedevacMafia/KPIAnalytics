KPI calculation rules
  • A unique case is identified by trip number, mission date and patient identifier; the newest export is retained.
  • Intubated means the airway is documented as ETT or endotracheal. Mechanical ventilation is measured separately.
  • Age is calculated from DOB on the mission date. Under one year means the mission occurred before the first birthday.
  • Reanimation medications mirrored into the main administration table are recognized through their source identifiers and are not counted twice.
  • Statistical vital-sign measures use one summarized value per case, so cases with more observations do not carry extra weight.
